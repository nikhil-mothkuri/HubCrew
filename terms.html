Terms of Service

Engagement standards and terms governing recruitment services provided by Hubcrew Talent Solutions LLP.

1. Scope of Mandates

All recruitment process outsourcing (RPO), recruiter pod deployments, and talent search engagements are executed under formal mandate agreements agreed upon following a scoping call.

2. Commercial Terms

Commercial structures—including success fees per hire, monthly retainers, and per-check fees—are finalized per mandate. Specific payment timelines and replacement or probation commitments are stipulated in individual service agreements.

3. Confidentiality

Both HubCrew and our client partners maintain mutual non-disclosure commitments regarding compensation structures, proprietary hiring scorecards, and candidate personal data.

4. Inquiries

For questions regarding terms of engagement, contact rini@hubcrewtalent.com.
